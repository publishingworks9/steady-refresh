A 30-second check catches missed reloads

Chrome puts idle extensions to sleep, and a lot of auto-refreshers lose their timer when that happens. The countdown keeps showing, but the page never reloads.

Steady Refresh keeps its schedule in Chrome's storage and checks every 30 seconds that each tab is on time. A missed turn gets made up, and a page that hangs while loading is tried again.

Pause, scroll and hours, one switch each

It waits while you're typing, keeps your place on the page, and only runs when you want it to. Pick 1 second to 24 hours, or type your own.

Start or stop from the keyboard with AltShiftR, or OptionShiftR on a Mac.

No account and no network requests

The text on your pages is checked on your own computer. Site access is asked for one site at a time, and only for the features that need it.

  • No accountInstall it and press Start.
  • No analytics or adsNothing measures how you use it.
  • No page text storedText is read in memory to check your alert, then let go.
